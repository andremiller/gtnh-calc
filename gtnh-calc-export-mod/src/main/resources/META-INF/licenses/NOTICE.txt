This mod bundles org.sejda.imageio:webp-imageio:0.1.6, a fork of Luciad's
WebP ImageIO plugin (Apache License 2.0):
https://github.com/sejda-pdf/webp-imageio
Copyright 2012 Luciad (http://www.luciad.com).

Its native encoder includes libwebp, copyright Google Inc., under the
BSD license and patent grant. The accompanying libwebp-copyright.txt
contains libwebp's license and attribution text as distributed by Debian.
The bundled plugin and native libraries retain their original packages
and resource paths because JNI depends on their names.
